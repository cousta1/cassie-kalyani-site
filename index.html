I found my magic on the mat.

When I walked into my first yoga class fifteen years ago, something shifted. The alignment, the breath, the poses — working together like a quiet spell. I kept coming back, kept searching for what that feeling was pointing to. At some point I knew I had to take the leap and train seriously. I'm glad I did. What I had been searching for emerged as a whole new world in front of me.

Yoga has been my anchor through many stages of my life. My safe place. And that time on the mat — no thoughts, no to-do lists, only love, grounding and light — is still the place where I listen to myself completely.

I have never stopped studying, and everything I learn finds its way into how I teach. I teach from my heart. What I offer my students is this: you will feel it from the first class. A body that moves more freely, a breath that steadies you. Over time, the practice becomes something you carry with you — better sleep, less stress, more presence. And beneath all of it, a connection between body, mind and spirit that deepens the longer you show up.

The door is always open. Come when you're ready.
