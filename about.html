Cassie Kalyāṇī

Hatha Yoga teacher, RYT-200. New York and online.

I teach classical Hatha Yoga as it was meant to be practiced: postures, breath, deep rest and meditation working together, so the body becomes steady and the mind grows quiet.

My training comes from the Integral Yoga Institute, one of the oldest yoga lineages in the West, rooted in the teachings of Swami Satchidananda. I have never stopped studying, and everything I learn finds its way into one practice.

My classes are trauma-informed and always adaptable. They will gently challenge you while offering a variation at every step, so the practice meets you where you are.

Why Kalyāṇī

Kalyāṇī is the Sanskrit spiritual name my teachers gave me. It means auspicious, beautiful and blessed.
